Oscar Best Picture Nominees vs. Ratings from 2010-2026

In 2010, the Academy Awards was coming off a historic ratings slide. The Oscars telecast had gone from a record high of 55 million viewers in 1998 (the year Titanic won best picture) to a low of 32 million viewers in 2008, and Academy president Sidney Ganis publicly worried that the Oscars were becoming too niche and inaccessible.

Hoping to capitalize on the future publicity of high-grossing movies like "The Dark Knight" and "Wall-E" (both of which were snubbed from the 2009 best picture nominations), Gains announced that, starting in 2010, the number of best picture nominees would be expanded from five to ten.

The question is: did it work?
